# SyncWire Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

SyncWire is a student project that turns meeting transcripts into tasks, emails the assignees and adds the deadlines to Google Calendar. This page explains what data it uses and why.

## What SyncWire accesses

When you sign in with Google, SyncWire asks for two permissions:

- **Gmail (send only):** to send meeting summaries and task emails from your account. SyncWire cannot read, search or delete your emails.
- **Google Calendar:** to create and update calendar events for task deadlines.

## What SyncWire stores

- Meeting transcripts you paste or upload, the AI-generated summary, and the extracted tasks (description, deadline, assignee email address, status).
- A private link token for each task, so assignees can update their own task.
- The Google access tokens needed to send email and create events. These are kept as protected server settings, not in the code.

## Who the data is shared with

Data is only passed to the services that make SyncWire work:

- **SambaNova**, which analyses the transcript text to extract tasks.
- **Supabase**, which hosts the database.
- **Microsoft Azure**, which hosts the application.
- **Google**, to send the emails and create the calendar events.

SyncWire does not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone else.

SyncWire's use of information received from Google APIs follows the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Your choices

- You can remove SyncWire's access at any time at <https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.
- To have your stored transcripts and tasks deleted, email the contact address below.
